How it works

A simple path from finding a resource to learning with it.

Explore openly shared materials, create a student account to save and contribute resources, and use the reading view when it suits your study style.

STEP 01

Explore

Search public materials by title, description or available extracted text. Open a resource to review its details.

STEP 02

Organize

Create an account to upload resources, save items to My Library and see materials shared privately with you.

STEP 03

Read or listen

Open the reading view for extracted text. If your browser supports speech synthesis, start playback and adjust the speed.

Sharing and visibility

When uploading, choose whether a resource is public or private. Public resources can be discovered by visitors. Private resources are intended for the owner and accounts the owner explicitly shares them with. You are responsible for having permission to upload and share the material.

Document support and limitations

PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, MD, RTF and CSV are accepted for upload. ODT, ODS and ODP may be accepted but do not currently have text extraction implemented. Scanned PDFs may need OCR. Extracted text is not a substitute for the original document.
