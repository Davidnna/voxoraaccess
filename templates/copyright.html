Rights and responsibilities

Copyright and content reports

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Voxora Access respects intellectual property rights. Users must upload and share only materials they own or are authorized to use and distribute.

Report material

If you believe material on Voxora Access infringes your copyright or another legal right, contact the platform operator with enough information to identify the material and assess the report.

Information to include

  • Your name and a reliable way to contact you.
  • The URL or resource title and enough detail to locate the material.
  • A description of the work or right you believe has been infringed.
  • An explanation of why you believe the use is unauthorized.
  • A statement that the information in your report is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
  • Evidence that you own the right or are authorized to act for the rights holder, where applicable.

Review and response

The operator may request more information, restrict access while reviewing a credible report, notify the uploader where appropriate, or restore material if a report is not substantiated. Submitting a report does not guarantee a particular outcome.

This page describes an operational reporting process, not a formal statutory notice-and-counter-notice procedure. Have the process reviewed and adapted to the laws applicable to the platform operator before launch.

Use the contact page to submit a report. Add a monitored rights-reporting email address before opening public uploads widely.
