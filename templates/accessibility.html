Accessibility at Voxora Access

Voxora Access aims to make learning resources easier to find, navigate and listen to.

Keyboard access

Use keyboard navigation, a skip link and visible focus indicators.

Speech output

Where supported, use the reading view and browser speech synthesis.

Readable layout

Semantic headings, labels, responsive spacing and reduced-motion support.

Known limitations

Extraction may omit diagrams, equations and reading order. Scanned PDFs need OCR.

This prototype has not been independently audited or certified against WCAG. Test with keyboard navigation, screen readers, magnification and real course materials.
