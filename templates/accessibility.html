Accessibility

Learning should work with the way you access information.

Voxora Access is designed with visually impaired university students in mind. We aim to make learning resources easier to discover, navigate and listen to.

NAVIGATION

Keyboard-friendly structure

Pages include semantic landmarks, labelled controls, a skip link and visible focus styles to support keyboard navigation.

READING

Listen to extracted text

The reading view can use browser speech synthesis, with playback controls and a speed setting where supported.

PRESENTATION

Readable layouts

Responsive layouts, clear headings and contrast-aware colours help make the interface easier to use across screen sizes.

Known limitations

  • Speech voices, language support and playback behaviour vary by browser, device and operating system.
  • Extracted text may omit images, diagrams, equations, complex tables or original reading order.
  • Scanned PDFs may not contain machine-readable text and may require OCR.
  • Some document formats are accepted for storage but do not currently have text extraction implemented.

Voxora Access has not been independently audited or certified against WCAG. Do not assume every page or uploaded document is fully accessible.

Tell us about an accessibility barrier

If you encounter a barrier, please contact the platform operator with the page, task, assistive technology and browser/device details if you can share them. See our Contact page.
