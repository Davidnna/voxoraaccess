Contact Voxora Access

Questions about using the platform, accessibility barriers, privacy concerns or a resource rights report should have a clear route to the platform operator.

Operator contact details need to be completed before public launch. Configure and publish a monitored support email address and the legal name and address of the platform operator. Do not rely on an unmonitored inbox for privacy or copyright reports.

What to include

  • For technical support: the page you were using, what happened and the browser/device involved.
  • For accessibility feedback: the task you were trying to complete and the barrier you encountered.
  • For privacy requests: the account email and the nature of your request. Do not send passwords.
  • For copyright reports: the resource link and the information listed on our copyright page.

For information about how account and resource information is handled, read the Privacy Notice.
