About Voxora Access

Learning resources should be easier to find, use and revisit.

Voxora Access is a student-focused learning-resource platform designed to make digital study materials more discoverable and more flexible to access.

OUR PURPOSE

Access with dignity

We want students, including people with visual impairments, to have more ways to engage with digital learning materials.

OUR APPROACH

Useful tools, clearly explained

Resource discovery, personal libraries, controlled sharing and browser-supported read-aloud tools sit in one place.

OUR COMMITMENT

Keep improving

Accessibility is ongoing work. We aim to make limitations visible and improve the experience through testing and feedback.

What the platform currently offers

  • Student accounts and a personal dashboard.
  • Public resource discovery and search.
  • Private uploads and sharing with registered accounts.
  • A personal library that saves references to resources.
  • Text extraction for supported document formats and a browser speech reading view.

Voxora Access is currently a learning-resource sharing prototype. Text extraction can omit images, diagrams, equations and complex document layout. Speech voices and availability depend on the browser and device.
